Favourite Thing I Have Recently Read

The favourite thing I've read as of late, as silly as it seems, is a comic book. Recently I read the first issue of Absolute Batman. I don't normally read comic books, but a friend kept recommending it to me. I gave in to the recommendations, and I wasn't disappointed.

This book reimagines Batman and his origins. Instead of both his parents dying, only his father is killed. Though? His origins seem tamer; he's brutal and more aggressive than a typical Batman. He's a hulking 7ft with his boots on, with a wide frame, and an intimidating bat symbol that almost looks like a fat rectangle with a subtle bat silhouette. Most of this issue goes back and forth between his past and the present, where he's trying to take down a highly gruesome and sadistic gang running rampant in Gotham: the Party Animals.

In this iteration, Bruce's (Batman) parents aren't incredibly rich, and Alfred is not their butler; instead has no part in Bruce's younger life. Alfred is a trained soldier who works for a mysterious group that wants to keep tabs on the party animals; because of this, Alfred and Bruce clash a bit and get to know each other.

Anyway, I won't say much more related to the story, but it was a very fun read. Even the villains have cool, new, unique designs. It's an interesting twist on the common iterations of Batman we know and love.
