Favourite Thing I Have Recently Read

The favourite thing ive read as of late, as silly as it seems is a comic book. Recently I read the first issue of Absolute Batman. I dont normally read comic books but a freind kept recomending me to read it. I gave in to the recomendations and I was not disapointed.

This book reimagines Batman and his origins. Instead of both his parents dying only his father is killed. Though his origins seem more tame hes brutal and more aggressive than typicall batman. Hes a hulking 7ft with his boots on, wide frame, and intimidating bat symbol that almost looks like a fat rectancle with a subtle sillouette of a bat. Most of this issue goes back and fourth between his past and the present where hes trying to take down a highly grousome and sadistic gang running rampid in Gotham, the party animals.

In this iteration Bruce's (Batman) parents arent incredibly rich, and Alfred not thier butler instead has no part in Bruce's younger life. Alfred is a trained solider who works for a mysterious group that want to keep tabs on the party animals. Because of this Alfred and Bruce clash a bit and get to know eachother.

Anyway I wont say much more related to the story but it was a very fun read. Even the villans have cool new unique designs. Its an interseting twist to the common iterations of batman we know and love.
